Who We Are

Engineering, under one roof.

Devji Industries is a mechanical engineering and manufacturing company based in MIDC Shiroli, Kolhapur, serving industrial customers across India since 1979.

From design and fabrication to CNC machining, foundry and assembly — we bring every capability together under one roof, giving our customers a single, dependable partner instead of a dozen vendors. Our diversified portfolio spans construction equipment, sugar & steel plant machinery, foundry castings and custom project work.
