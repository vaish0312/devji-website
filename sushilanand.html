About Us

Your dependable partner for construction equipment.

Sushilanand Equipments is a Kolhapur-based manufacturer and supplier of construction and material-handling equipment, offering the best range of mini construction lifts, rope-suspended platforms, concrete mixers, bar cutting & bending machines and goods lifts — with effective, timely delivery.

Backed by the engineering strength of the Devji group, we build rugged, reliable machines for builders and contractors across the region.
